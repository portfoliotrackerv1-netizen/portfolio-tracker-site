About Portfolio Tracker

A closer look
Experts

Sector calls and investment theses, ranked by conviction — written like something a research desk would hand you, not a generic tip. It's the one screen built to answer "where would you actually put money right now."

Portfolio Tracker is a single home for everything an investor in Indian equities needs to check day to day — holdings, corporate actions, IPOs and research — instead of piecing it together across five apps, screenshots and a spreadsheet.

It reads BSE and NSE data directly, keeps a running ledger of every position you hold, and surfaces the things that actually move a portfolio: a dividend about to be credited, a bonus issue you'd otherwise miss, an IPO closing in three days.

  • 01One ledger for holdings, corporate actions and IPOs — not five separate apps.
  • 02Research and fundamentals without leaving your portfolio view.
  • 03A PDF or Excel export whenever you need the numbers outside the app.
  • 04Built and maintained independently by someone who follows the market closely — and writes the code that tracks it.

Portfolio Tracker is a tracking and information tool — it doesn't offer investment advice or manage money on anyone's behalf.
